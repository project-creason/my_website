                  Getting started
                  
          
                  Your first scope takes about five minutes.
                  
          
                    
            
                  1. Type the prospect's company name, or load one of the example scenarios to see how a finished scope looks.
                    2. 
            
                  2. Answer the questions as you learn the answers on the call. You don't need them in order. Tap i on any question for what to listen for and what to ask next.
                    3. 
            
                  3. Watch the Checks panel. A warning means ask another question, or route the deal to a solutions engineer before quoting.
                    4. 
            
                  4. Read Why this scope so you can explain the plan to the buyer in one sentence.
                    5. 
            
                  5. Open Output and copy the buyer summary into your follow-up email, or the CRM payload into the deal.
                    6.

                  Seller and buyer views
                  
          
                  
            
                  ViewWho it's forWhat they see
                  
            
              
                  SellerReps and solutions engineers on a callAll questions with coaching tips, every check, the rule trace, list prices and the CRM output.
                  
              
                  Buyer: quick overviewExecutives deciding whether to look closerSix questions and an at-a-glance summary. Pricing follows the setting for this audience.
                  
              
                  Buyer: scoping a projectDirectors building a case for approvalAll questions, the full scope and a leadership package. Pricing follows the setting for this audience.
                  
            
                  
          
                  Buyers never see internal flags such as SE review or services gaps, or the rule trace.
                  
        
                  
        
                  Roles and access
                  
          
                  Reps can see the packaging but can't change it, and can discount up to the rep limit. Bigger discounts need a sales manager. RevOps admins edit packaging, rules and pricing visibility in a draft, then publish it to every rep at once. Each publish is recorded in the change log.
                  
          
                  
            
                  RoleCan do
                  
            
              
                  Sales repBuild scopes, share buyer summaries, view packaging and the change log.
                  
              
                  Sales managerEverything a rep can do, plus approve or decline discounts between the rep limit and the approval ceiling.
                  
              
                  RevOps adminEverything a rep can do, plus edit packaging, price books, discount limits and rules (including new rules from the rule builder), import catalog and CSV files, set what website visitors see, and publish.
                  
              
                  Solutions engineerplannedApprove deals flagged for SE review, and override a plan with a recorded reason.
                  
              
                  Finance approverplannedSecond approval before any price book change is published.
                  
              
                  PartnerplannedScope with a partner price book, limited to the plans that partner resells.
