The engineering approach to a trade business

After a decade in enterprise IT engineering (Neustar and Tealium), I founded Crestwood Home & Hearth in 2018 and treated it as a systems architecture problem: standardize multi-vendor data, automate quote-to-cash, instrument field diagnostics, and engineer unit economics before scaling headcount.

Counter-seasonal product & margin design

Traditional hearth businesses face steep warm-weather revenue drops. In Spring 2020, I expanded into 3D CAD-modeled automated pergolas, motorized smart screens, outdoor kitchens, and high-margin ceramic flue restoration—driving +310% year-over-year revenue growth, expanding average ticket size by +171%, and lifting peak gross margin to 63.6%.

4. Custom DOM/AJAX automation & quantitative diagnostics

Bypassing SaaS UI limits with JavaScript

When supplier pricebooks updated, hundreds of obsolete SKUs in Jobber needed to be purged before importing the new catalog. Because Jobber’s web interface lacked bulk deletion by vendor prefix, I wrote a browser-console jQuery/AJAX script (jobber_quick_product_delete.js) to scrape the internal item IDs from the DOM and fire asynchronous DELETE requests directly against Jobber’s endpoint:

// Production utility: Product Imports/jobber_quick_product_delete.js
var icc = $('a:contains("ICC")');
for (var i = 0; i < icc.length; i++) {
  var item = icc[i].href.split('/')[4];
  $.ajax({
    url: 'https://secure.getjobber.com/work_items/' + item,
    type: 'DELETE',
    success: function() { console.log('Removed', item); }
  });
}

This early catalog work directly inspired two standalone tools on this site: the Vendor Price Sheet → Jobber Import ETL pipeline and the Hearth Venting Planner.

Instrument-driven field diagnostics & SOPs

  • Millivolt gas valve ohmmeter testing: Authored technician training decks standardizing multimeter resistance checks across Robertshaw & SIT Electromagnet Power Units (0–13 Ω specification between EPU lead and TP terminal) and preventative pilot/spill-switch replacement rules to eliminate repeat callbacks.
  • Magnehelic differential pressure & airflow telemetry: Built CDET dryer exhaust evaluation forms recording pre- and post-cleaning backpressure (≤ 0.6" Water Column) and anemometer airflow velocity (MPH) at the appliance and exterior termination.
  • Time-critical chemical & refractory SOPs: Documented step-by-step field runbooks for ChamberGuard and FireGuard ceramic resurfacing equipment, including a strict 2-minute pump-blockage recovery decision tree before refractory material cures in the line.
  • M&A transition to a major local HVAC company: In 2022, packaged the 1,215-customer book (+419% growth), brand assets, inventory/tooling assessments, and operational playbooks into a clean asset sale and 3-year management integration agreement with a major local HVAC company.
