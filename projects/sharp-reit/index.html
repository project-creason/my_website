SHARP: Senior Housing Acquisition & Regional Platform

Senior housing and skilled nursing acquisitions represent one of the most complex, regulated asset classes in commercial real estate: Certificate of Need (CON) state moats, Medicaid statutory shortfalls, CMS staffing mandates, volatile clinical wage indices, and looming debt refinancing walls. Traditional REIT underwriting addresses these challenges through fragmented spreadsheets, static broker packages, and weeks of manual due diligence. SHARP unifies the entire acquisition pipeline into an automated quantitative operating terminal evaluating 14,686 CMS-certified nursing facilities across all 3,144 U.S. counties with dynamic 5-year NNN vs. RIDEA DCF pro formas and 1-click Investment Committee memos.

The institutional problem

  • Fragmented operating bench: Over 60% of facilities nationally are held by non-chain "mom-and-pop" operators, creating massive off-market consolidation upside alongside operational opacity.
  • Regulatory barriers to entry: State Certificate of Need (CON) laws and construction moratoria govern the supply of licensed beds, dictating whether new competitors can enter a market.
  • Statutory reimbursement drags: High Medicaid utilization exposes operators to statutory reimbursement shortfalls (often -$20 to -$100/resident/day below actual care costs), requiring precise private-pay qualification modeling.
  • Labor & clinical inflation: Volatile agency staffing rates and CMS clinical wage discrepancies create substantial operating margin shocks.
  • Commercial debt maturity walls: High-leverage floating-rate debt originated in low-rate environments faces refinancing shocks and DSCR compression, creating motivated off-market sellers.

The 5 core capability pillars

  • 1. National spatial intelligence: Evaluates all 3,144 U.S. counties, filtering for institutional scale (total beds ≥ 300, 75+ pop ≥ 2,000, ≥ 2 facilities → 1,026 rankable submarkets) across 5 strategy presets (Value-Add Turnaround, Core-Plus Stable Yield, Mom-and-Pop Roll-Up, High-Barrier Infill, Distressed Workout).
  • 2. Target asset screener: Verified CMS regulatory sourcing tracking Medicare payment denials, Special Focus Facility (SFF) active enforcement (87) vs. candidate watchlists (440), and commercial debt maturity clocks.
  • 3. 5-year DCF underwriting engine: Side-by-side DCF modeling comparing Triple-Net (NNN) Master Leases against RIDEA Joint Ventures (TRS structures), incorporating state CON exit cap compression, BLS clinical wage index multipliers, and Medicaid daily rate drags.
  • 4. Investment Committee (IC) automation: Dynamically generates 4-page institutional PDF underwriting memos formatted for executive review, complete with CMS PBJ nurse staffing compliance audits and 50-mile replacement operator benches.
  • 5. Acquisition Deal Room & Cart: 6-stage Kanban transaction board (Sourced → Active Underwriting → IC Review → LOI Executed → Due Diligence → Closed Portfolio) with a multi-asset portfolio cart calculating blended entry basis, Year-1 NOI, and HHI geographic diversification.

Technical architecture

  • Local-first high performance: Engineered in Python 3.11+, DuckDB, and Parquet columnar storage for instant querying of 14,000+ facilities without heavy database overhead.
  • Full data transparency: 100% of demographic, housing, and rating imputations are tracked via explicit boolean flags (is_income_imputed, is_home_value_imputed, is_rating_imputed, has_imputed_data) and displayed in the UI.
  • Automated monthly pipeline: GitHub Actions workflow that automatically refreshes Census, CMS, and FEMA datasets, executes the transformation pipeline, runs test verification, and updates versioned Parquet data snapshots.
  • Streamlit frontend: Responsive dark/light theme terminal with interactive Plotly geographic choropleths, dual pro forma DCF calculators, and PDF memo generation.
