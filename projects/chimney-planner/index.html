Hearth Venting Planner

Planning a wood-stove or fireplace chimney means cross-referencing a thick manufacturer catalog, clearance rules, and NFPA 211. This planner does it for you: pick a manufacturer and venting system, describe the installation, and it returns every part you need, with quantities and part numbers.

  • NFPA 211 checked
  • Multi-manufacturer
  • Part numbers & quantities
  • Flags problems early
  • CSV export
Launch the planner

What it does

  • Lets you choose a manufacturer and venting system (currently DuraVent DuraTech and ICC EXCEL, 5″–8″) and switches rules and parts automatically while keeping your measurements.
  • Walks you through the appliance, ceiling, attic, and roof measurements, or loads a worked example to start from.
  • Applies the strictest rule among the manufacturer’s installation manual, NFPA 211, and your appliance manual’s own limits.
  • Builds the parts list by location (ceiling, chimney pipe, attic, roof, top), flags anything to double-check, and exports to CSV or a priced dealer order.

Why it matters

  • Turns hours of cross-checking a catalog, a code book, and an appliance manual into a few minutes of measurements.
  • Never shorts a requirement: heights and clearances can be exceeded but never undercut, and pipe lengths always round up.
  • Catches problems before anything is bought, like an offset that won’t fit in the attic or a chase too wide for stock flashing.
  • Gets the order right the first time, with exact part numbers, quantities, and optional retailer/distributor SKU and price mapping.
  • Works for any stove or fireplace: you enter what its manual requires, so the planner never falls behind new models.

Built for

  • Homeowners planning an install and wanting to know what it takes before they start.
  • Installers and sweeps quoting jobs quickly and consistently.
  • Hearth retailers and distributors building a complete, priced order with their own SKUs at the counter.

Coming next

  • Offsets on through-the-wall installations
  • Free-standing stove placement and stovepipe
  • Additional residential chimney, pellet, and direct-vent product lines
  • AI that reads an appliance manual and fills in its requirements

A planning aid, not an installation approval. Follow local code and have the finished system inspected by a certified professional.
