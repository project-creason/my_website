Louisville Crime: 2024 vs. 2025

I analyzed two full years of Louisville Metro Police Department (LMPD) incident reports (69,876 offenses occurring in 2024 and 70,336 in 2025) alongside daily NOAA weather observations and lunar phases to see which crime patterns hold steady year over year and where 2025 shifted.

Core findings: 2024 vs. 2025

  • Temperature vs. rain (consistent)Daily high temperature showed a nearly identical positive correlation in both years: r = 0.514 in 2024 (+0.73 offenses/°F) and r = 0.511 in 2025 (+0.76 offenses/°F). Rain alone barely moved daily totals (-0.8% on rainy days in 2024; -4.7% in 2025).
  • Lunar influence (consistent)Full moons still show no positive effect. The 12 full-moon days averaged 193.7/day vs. 190.8/day in 2024 (+1.5%), and 181.8/day vs. 193.1/day in 2025 (-5.9%).
  • Seasonality (consistent)Q3 (Jul–Sep) led both years (18,953 in 2024; 19,245 in 2025), with July as the peak month (6,534 and 6,631) and February as the lowest (5,094 and 4,814).
  • Geography (core held, edges shifted)West and central ZIPs 40203 (4,933 → 4,929) and 40211 (4,772 → 4,889) stayed #1 and #2, while Downtown 40202 fell 20.2% (4,078 → 3,256) and southwest/east areas like 40272 (+11.5%), 40216 (+7.1%), and 8th Division (+12.1%) rose.
  • Offense mix (diverged sharply)Total volume was flat (+0.7%), but auto theft plunged 20.1% (5,769 → 4,608), homicide fell 26.1% (153 → 113), robbery fell 18.4% (971 → 792), and burglary fell 11.5% (3,517 → 3,113) — offset by surges in shoplifting (+28.5%), narcotics (+21.9%), and trespassing (+39.9%).
  • Venue shifts (parking down, retail up)Parking lot and garage offenses fell 11.9% (7,900 → 6,963) alongside the auto-theft drop, while grocery/supermarket incidents jumped 73.6% (818 → 1,420) and department/discount stores rose 19.6% (1,941 → 2,322).
  • Day of week (shifted to weekend)Mondays led 2024 (10,519 offenses), ahead of Saturday (10,376). In 2025, activity shifted to the weekend: Saturday (#1 at 10,446) and Friday (#2 at 10,398) overtook Monday (#4 at 9,984).
  • Holidays & severe weather (new low)Thanksgiving was the lowest day of 2024 (112) and dropped to 150 in 2025 (Christmas fell to 133). The quietest day across both years was Jan 6, 2025 (88 offenses) during a 7+ inch snow and ice storm.
  • Peak days & 1st-of-month effectJuly 22 topped 2024 (269). In 2025, July 1 (276), Aug 1 (274), and June 1 (273) led raw counts, followed by May 30 (272) — driven by retroactive reports that default to the 1st of the month (13.0% reported >7 days later vs. 3.9% on other days).

Completion rate: Completed offenses accounted for 76.3% of reports in 2024 (53,297 completed vs. 16,579 attempted/non-completed) and 74.6% in 2025 (52,485 completed vs. 17,851 attempted/non-completed).

What held steady across both years

  • Heat drives crime up linearly; rain barely matters: Daily maximum temperature explained ~26% of daily variance in both years (r = 0.514 and r = 0.511), adding roughly 3 more offenses for every +4°F warming, while rainy days (≥0.1″) moved daily totals by less than 5%.
  • Full moons don’t move the needle: Across 24 full moons in 2024–2025, full-moon days averaged 187.7 offenses/day versus 191.9/day on all other days.
  • Summer Q3 peak, winter Q1 trough: July and August were the #1 and #2 months in both years, while February was the lowest month in both years.
  • Geographic concentration: The top four ZIP codes (40203, 40211, 40214, 40219) held the exact same 1–4 rank order in both 2024 and 2025, accounting for 27% of all citywide offenses.
  • Retroactive 1st-of-the-month reporting lag: In both years, the 1st of the month averaged 16–26% more offenses than days 2–31 because delayed reports (13.0% reported >7 days later in 2025 vs. 3.9% on other days) frequently default to the 1st.

What shifted in 2025

  • Auto theft, homicide, robbery, and burglary dropped sharply: Auto theft fell 20.1% (-1,161), homicide fell 26.1% (-40), robbery fell 18.4% (-179), and burglary fell 11.5% (-404), while retail shoplifting (+28.5%), narcotics (+21.9%), and trespassing (+39.9%) surged.
  • Venue mix moved from parking lots to retail aisles: Parking lot and garage offenses dropped 11.9% (-937), while grocery/supermarket incidents rose 73.6% (+602) and department/discount stores rose 19.6% (+381).
  • Downtown dropped while outer-ring ZIPs grew: Downtown (40202) fell by 822 offenses (-20.2%), while Valley Station (40272, +11.5%), Shively (40216, +7.1%), and LMPD’s 8th Division (+12.1%) saw higher volume.
  • Weekend peak replaced the Monday peak: Saturday and Friday became the busiest days of the week in 2025, while Monday fell from #1 to #4 (-5.1%).
  • Severe winter weather beat holidays for the quietest day: While Thanksgiving (150) and Christmas (133) stayed quiet in 2025, the January 6, 2025 snow and ice storm brought citywide reports down to just 88 offenses.
